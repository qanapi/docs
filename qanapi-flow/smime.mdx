# Google S/MIME Setup Steps
1. Enable the [Admin SDK API](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/google/admin.googleapis.com)
    1. Click the "Enable" button and wait for it to finish, where it will redirect you to the SDK API management page.
    2. If you already see a "Manage" button, this is already enabled
2. Enable [Gmail SDK API](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/google/gmail.googleapis.com)
    1. Click the "Enable" button and wait for it to finish, where it will redirect you to the SDK API management page.
    2. If you already see a "Manage" button, this is already enabled
3. Enable [S/MIME for Gmail](https://admin.google.com/ac/apps/gmail/usersettings)
    1. Click the section at the bottom for "S/MIME"
    2. Check "Enable S/MIME encryption for sending and receiving emails"
    3. Check "Allow users to upload their own certificates"
    4. Click "Save"
4. Enable [Client-Side Encryption for Gmail](https://admin.google.com/ac/cse/keyservice) (if using CSE with S/MIME)
    1. In the "Assignment" section, under "Encryption status by app", click "Edit" for Gmail
    2. Select "On" and click "Save"

# Ordering Certificates for Users
1. Select S/MIME (/flow/smime) in the Qanapi App
2. Click the "Manage" link on the Provider
3. Select the non-active users you wish to enable by clicking the checkbox next to each one
4. Click the "Enable" button
5. Certificates will be ordered for each selected user, and the status will change to "Pending Order"
6. Once the order has processed in the background, the status will change to "Pending Approval" and the user will receive an email to verify their email address
7. Users will follow the link in the email and click the "Approve" button
8. The status will change to "Pending Upload", and the app will attempt to upload the certificate (or CSE Identity) to Gmail. This may take up to 5 minutes
9. After a successful upload, the status will change to "Active"

<Note>You may view a user's Gmail certificate information by clicking the "Certificate Info" button next to a user on the Qanapi S/MIME management page</Note>

## Cancelling Certificates for Users
1. Select S/MIME (/flow/smime) in the Qanapi App
2. Click the "Manage" link on the Provider
3. Select the active users you wish to disable by clicking the checkbox next to each one
4. Click the "Disable" button
5. Users will have their status updated to "Pending Cancellation"
6. The certificate (or CSE Identity) will be removed from Gmail

# Sending a signed email
1. In Gmail, open a new draft email by clicking the "Compose" button
2. Click the lock icon (<Icon icon="lock" color="gray"/>) in the top right of the email
3. Under "Digital Signature", click the "Sign Message" option
4. Draft the email and click "Send"
5. Once sent, the email should have a certificate icon (<Icon icon="award" color="#1A73E8"/>) under the sender's name

# Encrypting emails
In order to send encrypted emails with another email address, both senders must first send signed emails in order to exchange public certificates.
1. In Gmail, draft and send a signed email to the desired recipient
2. The recipient must do the same, replying to the first email with their own signed email
3. Begin a reply to this email, and click the lock icon (<Icon icon="lock" color="gray"/>) in the top right of the email
4. Under the "Additional encryption" section, click the "Turn on" link. A verification dialog may open warning about the loss of any existing body to the email
5. Draft the email and click "Send". This may take a few seconds as Gmail encrypts the email
6. Once sent, the email should have a green lock icon (<Icon icon="lock"/>) or blue shield icon (<Icon icon="shield" color="#1A73E8"/>) under the sender's name

All future replies to this email chain will now be encrypted with the recipient

<Note>A green lock icon denotes Hosted S/MIME, where Gmail uses certificates uploaded from Qanapi.<br/>A blue shield icon denotes CSE-wrapped S/MIME, where Gmail uses Qanapi's Client-Side Encryption</Note>
