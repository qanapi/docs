# How to purchase

<Note>
    Before starting, you will need to have a Qanapi account.  You can use the
[quickstart guide](/get-started/quickstart) to get started.
</Note>

<Note>
    For CSE and Live Redaction you will need an Encryption Configuration as well.  The above Quickstart walks you through
    creating one as the second step.
</Note>

<Steps>
    <Step title="Create Your Provider Configuration">
        Once logged in, you'll need to create a Provider Configuration:

        1. Go to the <Icon icon="puzzle-piece" color="#08A046" iconType="solid"/> <b>Configurations</b> > <b>Providers</b>
        2. Click <Icon icon="plus" color="#08A046" iconType="solid"/> <b>Add Provider</b>
        1. Select either OAuth or Service Account for your authentication method.
        2. Fill out the form with your provider's credentials.
        3. Click <Badge color="green" shape="pill">Create & Save</Badge> or <Badge color="green" shape="pill">Authenticate with Google</Badge>
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click the Purchase Keys Button">
        1. Now that you have your provider, you can purchase a bundle.
            1. You can do this from:
                1. Any page under <Icon icon="briefcase" color="#08A046" iconType="solid"/> <b>Qanapi Flow</b>.
                1. From the <b>Billing</b> page.
                1. On your new Provider in the <b>Providers</b> page.
        3. Click the <Badge color="green" shape="pill">
        <Icon icon="cart-shopping" color="#ffffff" iconType="solid"/> Purchase Keys</Badge>.
        4. Select your Provider (unless coming from the Providers page) and then your bundle.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Complete Stripe Purchase">
        1. Fill out your billing details with Stripe.
        2. Complete the purchase.
        3. You will be redirected to your Provider details page.
        4. Once the Stripe order finishes, your new keys will appear.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Setup each key">
        1. For each key you purchased through the bundle, you will need to configure them.
        2. Under <Icon icon="briefcase" color="#08A046" iconType="solid"/> <b>Qanapi Flow</b> you will see links for each
        product.
        3. Click through each product you purchased.
            1. If they require an Encryption Configuration, you must assign it first.
            2. Click the <Badge color="grey"><Icon icon="cog" color="#fff" iconType="solid" /> Setup Guide</Badge> button
            and follow the steps to fully set up the product in your Google Org.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Enjoy your new products">
        That's it!  You are now ready to use Qanapi Flow products!
    </Step>
</Steps>
