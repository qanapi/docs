# What is it?

The Guided Setup is a guided walkthrough to help setting up any bundle or trial your account may require.  If you purchase
any of our bundles, the setup will guide you through setting them up all at once.  It groups the steps by
where they need to be completed (i.e.; Google Admin Console vs. Google Cloud Platform Console).  This is meant to get
the products up and running quickly.

<Note>
    Before starting, you will need to have a Qanapi account.  You can use the
    [quickstart guide](/get-started/quickstart) to get started.  You will also need to start a trial or [purchase a
    bundle](/qanapi-flow/purchase) for this area to show.
</Note>


<Steps>
    <Step title="Go to the Guided Setup Page">
        1. In your account, once you have started the trial or purchased a bundle, a new page will appear in the left side
        navigation.
        2. Click the <Icon icon="route" color="#08A046" iconType="solid" />&nbsp;<b>Guided Setup</b> link.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Setup Configurations">
        1. You will need both a provider configuration and an encryption configuration.
        2. The [quickstart guide](/get-started/quickstart) will walk you through the Encryption Configuration.
        3. The [purchase guide](/qanapi-flow/purchase) will walk you through the Provider Configuration.
        4. If your bundle included S/MIME, you <b>MUST</b> add a service account json to the Provider Configuration.
            1. There is a guide for how to do this in the Guided Setup.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Go through each step">
        1. Go through each step in order to get set up with the products.
        2. Some steps will validate your information with Google.  Not all steps can be validated from the API.
        3. Once all steps are completed, the <Badge color="green" shape="pill">Mark Setup Completed</Badge> button will
        be enabled.
        4. Click on it to complete the setup.
            1. If your bundle included S/MIME, a window will pop up to give you extra options..
    </Step>
</Steps>
