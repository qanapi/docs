# Qanapi API Documentation

Qanapi is an advanced API-based encryption service designed to provide granular control over your sensitive data. As a third-party key management service, Qanapi enables organizations to encrypt and decrypt data with ease, while securely managing encryption keys.

## What is Qanapi?

Qanapi offers robust data classification features, allowing you to control who can access specific parts of your data based on classifications and access groups. With quantum-hardened encryption and FIPS 140-3 compliant security, Qanapi provides enterprise-grade data protection while maintaining flexibility and ease of use.
